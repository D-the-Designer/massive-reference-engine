Marine Life & Sea Monsters

A growing collection of sea-creature illustrations and photos, licensed Public Domain or Creative Commons, ready to drop into a site or publication. Drag any image straight out of this page and into another browser window, a canvas app like Adobe Firefly Boards or Figma, a document, or onto your desktop — it carries the image at your selected resolution plus its attribution. Dragging also copies the image itself to your clipboard, so if an app won't accept the drop, just paste (Ctrl/Cmd+V). Every card lists its exact license and links straight back to the Wikimedia Commons source page — click Copy for a ready-to-paste attribution that includes the license and source link.
